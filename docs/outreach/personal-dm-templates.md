**Cap: 30 min, 15-20 sends total.** Pick from these templates based on relationship type. Don't send the same one twice in the same group chat — Apple/Meta/IG flag bulk-pattern texts.

## Targets (in priority order)

1. **Anyone you know who hosts on Airbnb** — even one property. Highest conversion.
2. **Friends who invested in or know STR investors** — they have hosts in their network.
3. **Founders in your Slack/Telegram groups** — community signal lifts trust.
4. **Real estate agents you know** — they know investor-hosts.
5. **Family members** — they'll forward to actual hosts they know.

## Post-send

- Reply within 5 minutes to anyone who responds. Speed of reply > polish of reply.
- If they say "I don't host but know X who does" — get the intro, write a personal line for X.
- If they ask "what's the 50% off thing" — paste: `restay.agency/?promo=FLASH50` and let the cookie middleware do the rest.

## What NOT to send

- Generic LinkedIn DMs to non-friends. Auto-flagged as spam.
- Mass-broadcast to a group of 50+ people. Looks bot-like.
- Any message that doesn't reference something specific about the recipient. Personalized > volume.
