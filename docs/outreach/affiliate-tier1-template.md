# Affiliate Tier-1 — 10 personalized cold-email drafts

The 10 highest-leverage voices in `{{NICHE}}` who can move the most ICP-attention to `{{MERCHANT}}`. Each draft below is personalized to a specific recipient; do **not** templatize Tier-1 — they will see right through a fill-in-the-blank.

**Send from operator's personal address**, not `partners@{{MERCHANT}}.tld`. Tier-1 must look 1:1.

Goal: 3+ replies of 10. < 2 = sharpen the pitch.

> **Reference:** Restay's filled-in version lives at [`Zilla-HQ/airbnb/docs/outreach/affiliate-tier1.md`](https://github.com/Zilla-HQ/airbnb/blob/main/docs/outreach/affiliate-tier1.md) — Sean Rakidzich, Robuilt, Rusteen, Jasper Ribbers, TFV, Faeth, Avery Carl, Chang, Symon He, Lodgify drafts.

## Template structure for each draft

```
Subject: {{Specific concrete hook from their recent content}}

Hi {{First name}},

{{ONE-SENTENCE specific reference to a recent thing they made — a podcast episode, a post, a course launch}}.

{{Two sentences on what {{MERCHANT}} does, framed in their language. Not "we help X do Y" — "{{name your specific work}}"}}.

{{One sentence on what we want. Not "join our affiliate program" — "would you be open to a {{specific offer}} for your {{audience name}}?"}}

{{Sender first name}}
```

The friction-cutting rules:
- **No "Hi I hope this finds you well."** Open with the specific hook from your concrete reference. They get 50 of these a day; only specificity gets read.
- **No more than 4 short sentences.** Anything longer reads like a SaaS pitch. Length signals junk mail.
- **Don't attach anything.** They will not click an attachment. Link in-line if there's a single must-see asset.
- **Ask the simplest possible thing.** Not "let's set up a call to discuss a partnership" — "would you be open to a `{{specific offer}}`?" One question, one answer needed.
