# Sending notes

- **Send Thursday or Friday morning.** Podcast sponsor inboxes are emptiest end-of-week.
- **Track replies, not opens** — sponsor inquiry inboxes are usually shared and bots will skew opens.
- **If they respond:** before negotiating, ask for the past-3-episode download numbers and any sponsor-conversion data they're willing to share. Helps you anchor the rate.
- **Bench rate:** for an Airbnb-host audience the implicit CPM tends to be $25–40 for a 60s host-read. A 12k-download episode at $30 CPM = ~$360. The pricing above is a premium of that because Restay's CAC is unusually friendly to one-time pricing.
- **Decision rule:** if a single 1-episode sponsor pulls 4 paid Tune-Ups (4 × $79 × ~70% margin = ~$220 contribution), you're at break-even at $1,500. The decision math is "is this audience likely to convert >2× that?" — if yes, sponsor; if no, pass.
