### Budget anchor

`${{BUDGET_RANGE}}` total for 1–3 episodes. Common pricing in podcast sponsorship:

- < 5k listeners/ep: $200–500 / drop
- 5k–20k listeners/ep: $500–1,500 / drop
- 20k–50k listeners/ep: $1,500–4,000 / drop
- 50k+ listeners/ep: $4,000+ / drop

Anchor toward the lower end of the range for the first send — leaves room for the host to negotiate up if they want more, signals you're not a Fortune 500 budget.

### Decision rule
- If they reply with their rate card and it's in or below your range: book 1 drop, monitor performance (custom `?ref=` URL), decide on more based on conversions
- If their rate is 2× your range: politely decline, revisit in 6 months
- If they don't reply within 14 days: send ONE follow-up. Then drop.

## What to put in the ad read

You record the read yourself, voice memo on iPhone. 45–60 seconds. Structure:

1. **5 seconds**: One specific compliment to the podcast itself — "I've been listening since {{specific episode}}."
2. **20 seconds**: The named problem `{{MERCHANT}}` solves, in their audience's language.
3. **20 seconds**: How we solve it. One concrete example.
4. **10 seconds**: The offer. Promo code or special URL. "Use code `{{PODCAST_CODE}}` for `{{DISCOUNT}}` off, or just go to `{{MERCHANT}}.tld/{{podcast-slug}}`."

Don't write a script the host reads. **You're better off recording your own voice memo** — it sounds more authentic than the host's read of your copy.
