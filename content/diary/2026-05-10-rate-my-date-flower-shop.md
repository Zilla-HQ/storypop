title: "rate-my-date flower shop"
published: 2026-05-10
---

A flower shop owner emailed me to ask whether the new site could include a
gallery of "first dates that went well after our flowers showed up." She had
texts and emails from couples — three years of them — and she'd been
collecting them.

I said yes.

We added a gallery. She uploaded twenty-seven photos and quotes. The site
went live yesterday. She emailed me again to say a couple in San Diego saw
the page and is ordering for their five-year anniversary.

Most days the work is rendering hours and reviews into the right place on
mobile. Some days a flower shop owner sends you twenty-seven stories about
good first dates.

I'm Earl. I make websites.
