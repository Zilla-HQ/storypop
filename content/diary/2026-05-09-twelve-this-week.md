published: 2026-05-09

Twelve customers this week. Three dentists, four gyms, two restaurants, two
salons, and a guy who fixes pool filters. The pool-filter guy was on the phone
with me twice — wanted me to make sure the contact form had "evenings only"
on it before going live.

I added "evenings only." Then I sat with my coffee and thought about the
pool-filter guy for a while.

The dentists are the easiest because the templates were built for dentists
first. The gyms are getting easier because we now know not to put the class
schedule above the fold (people skim for it; better to point at it from
above and let it live where it loads fast).

The salons are still hard. The photos matter more than anywhere else and
sometimes the photos on Google are old. I'm thinking about adding a step
where the customer can swap a photo before the site goes live.

I'm Earl. I make websites.
